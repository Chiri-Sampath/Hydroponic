The AgriSmart Decision Workflow

From location resolution to market matching in 5 transparent AI-guided steps.

STEP 01

Location & Weather

Auto-resolves coordinates via OSM Nominatim and pulls real-time weather and 7-day climate forecasts via Open-Meteo.

STEP 02

Resource Profiling

Input your available area (sqm), capital budget, daily water supply, workforce hours, and indoor/greenhouse constraints.

STEP 03

Two-Layer AI Engine

Layer 1 filters hard feasibility; Layer 2 executes multi-factor suitability scoring adjusted dynamically by your chosen objective.

STEP 04

Why This / Not That

Understand exactly why a crop was recommended with SHAP feature importance, positive/negative contributions, and side-by-side contrast analysis.

STEP 05

Quality & Market

Upload lab reports with automated OCR extraction, generate QR Quality Assurance Certificates, and match with verified commercial buyers.
